Most problems with streaming to a HomePod come from the network, not from the program. A short checklist:

## The speaker is not in the list

Make sure the computer and the speaker are on the **same network** and press refresh. If your network blocks automatic discovery (some routers, VLANs), add the speaker by its IP address in the settings.

## There is no sound

- Allow airhome through **Windows Firewall** for private networks when Windows asks. Without that there is no sound.
- In the speaker's AirPlay settings choose "Everyone on the same network" and make sure **no AirPlay password** is set.
- Network filters, for example AdGuard's network driver, can block the connection. Try turning them off to check.

## The sound is delayed

AirPlay always adds some delay of its own. Choose **Video** or **Games** mode on the main screen: the delay to the ear drops from about 700 ms to about 300 and 260 ms (measured with a 100 ms buffer on a HomePod mini). A little is left, so in a player such as VLC or MPC-HC shift the sound earlier by about that amount. This matters for video and games, where the picture must match the sound.

## The sound is choppy

Weak Wi-Fi is the usual reason. Raise the buffer, or move the speaker or the router closer. A larger buffer makes playback steadier at the cost of a little more delay.
