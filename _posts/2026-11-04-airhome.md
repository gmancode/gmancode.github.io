Windows has no built-in way to send its sound to a HomePod. I made a small tray program for that.

It captures everything the computer plays and sends it over your local network to the speaker using the AirPlay 2 protocol. While it streams, the sound on the computer itself is muted and comes back on its own afterwards. You can play on several speakers at once and change the volume from the tray menu.

The program is free and open source. The source code and the installer are [on GitHub](https://github.com/gmancode/airhome).
